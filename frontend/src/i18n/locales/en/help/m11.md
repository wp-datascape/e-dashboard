Measures the **stickiness** of the customer base: of the customers who were actively transacting in the previous period, what percentage are still actively transacting in this period.

## Formula

`Customers who were Active/Reactivated in the previous period AND ARE STILL Active/Reactivated this period ÷ Customers who were Active/Reactivated in the previous period × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Cohort (previous period)**: All customers who were in Active Customer or Reactivated status in the period BEFORE the measurement period. This population is the fixed baseline (denominator) — new customers or customers who just reactivated in the current period do NOT add to this cohort. See the Glossary above.
- **Retained**: From the previous period's cohort, customers who are STILL Active Customer or Reactivated this period.
- **Lost**: From the previous period's cohort, customers who are NO LONGER Active Customer or Reactivated this period (moved to Lapsed or Dormant).

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means customers who were already active tend to keep transacting in the following period — a stable customer base.
- **A low number** means many active customers stop transacting in the following period, even if the total active customer count looks stable (masked by new or other reactivated customers).
- New customers (Acquisition) or customers who just reactivated this period do NOT affect this period's Retention Rate — they only start counting toward the cohort from the following period onward.

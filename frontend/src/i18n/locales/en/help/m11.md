Measures the **stickiness** of the customer base: of the customers who were Existing Active in the previous period, what percentage are still Existing Active in this period.

## Formula

`Existing Active customers from the previous period who are STILL Existing Active this period ÷ Existing Active customers from the previous period × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Existing Active**: Customers in Active Customer or Reactivated status — the SAME term and population used by M3-M6 (see the Glossary above). The previous period's Existing Active population is the fixed baseline (denominator) — customers who just became Active/Reactivated this period do NOT add to that baseline; they only start counting as a baseline from the following period onward.
- **Retained Customers**: From the previous period's Existing Active, customers who are STILL Active Customer or Reactivated this period.
- **Lost Customers**: From the previous period's Existing Active, customers who are NO LONGER Active Customer or Reactivated this period (moved to Lapsed or Dormant).
- **New Transactions**: From this period's Existing Active, customers who were NOT part of the previous period's Existing Active — for example, a customer who was Lapsed and transacted again, or was Dormant and got Reactivated. Shown in the chart tooltip, not on the summary cards.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means customers who were already active tend to keep transacting in the following period — a stable customer base.
- **A low number** means many active customers stop transacting in the following period, even if the total Existing Active count looks stable (masked by New Transactions).
- New customers (Acquisition) never affect the Retention Rate — the Existing Active population deliberately excludes Acquisition.

Measures the **economic value** of retained customers and the effectiveness of retention and account-growth strategy: the average revenue generated from existing customers who are still active.

## Formula

`Total Revenue from Existing Active ÷ Number of Existing Active`

## Term Definitions

- **Total Revenue from Existing Active**: Total revenue generated from customers who have transacted before and are still active within the measurement period.
- **Existing Active**: Customers who have transacted before and are still actively transacting this period (Active Customer + Reactivated). See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A rising number** shows increasing value per customer, could be from up-selling, cross-selling, or higher order frequency/size.
- **A falling number** may indicate declining purchases, some customers starting to churn, or price pressure.

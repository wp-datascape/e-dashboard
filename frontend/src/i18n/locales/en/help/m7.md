Measures the success of the **account growth strategy**: the percentage growth in business value from existing customers who are not yet considered lost, through higher purchases, more product categories, or larger transaction volume.

## Formula

`Customers with Increased Revenue ÷ Customer Base (Addressable) × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Customer Base (Addressable)**: Customers who have transacted before and have not yet crossed the dormant threshold. Not required to have transacted this period (unlike Existing Active). See the Glossary above.
- **Customers with Increased Revenue**: Customers within the Customer Base (Addressable) population whose purchase value this period is higher than the previous period.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means account management is working effectively and the customer relationship keeps growing.
- **A low number** means customers tend to be stagnant, with account potential not yet fully tapped.

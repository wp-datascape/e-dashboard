Measures the effectiveness of the **reactivation strategy** and how successfully lapsed customers are recovered: the percentage of dormant customers (out of the entire customer base) who successfully transact again.

## Formula

`Reactivated (previously Dormant) ÷ Dormant at Start of Period × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Reactivated**: A customer who was previously Dormant, then made at least 1 transaction again within the measurement period. See the Glossary above.
- **Dormant**: All customers who were in Dormant status at the start of the measurement period. See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means follow-up strategy, engagement, and win-back campaigns are working effectively.
- **A low number** means customers are hard to recover, or the reactivation approach is not well targeted.

Measures how successfully the company drives sales of **high-margin products**: the percentage of existing active customers who buy products with a larger profit contribution.

## Formula

`Existing Active Buying High Margin Products ÷ Existing Active × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Existing Active Buying High Margin Products**: The number of existing active customers who made at least one purchase in a product category with a gross margin above the company-defined threshold.
- **Existing Active**: Customers who have transacted before and are still actively transacting this period (Active Customer + Reactivated). See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means the sales team is successfully steering customers toward more profitable products.
- **A low number** means customers are still predominantly buying low-margin products.

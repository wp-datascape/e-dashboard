Measures **purchase depth**: the average number of product categories each active customer buys in a period.

## Formula

`Total Categories Sold ÷ Active Transacting`

## Term Definitions

- **Total Categories Sold**: The accumulated number of unique product categories purchased by all customers within a given period.
- **Active Transacting**: All customers who transacted in this period, including first-time customers (see the Glossary above).

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A rising number** usually signals effective bundling, successful up-selling/cross-selling by the sales team, or stronger customer engagement.
- **The higher** the number, the better the product penetration into the customer base.

Measures the rate of customers who have stopped transacting and the **potential revenue loss** from the entire customer base: the percentage of customers who were previously active but have not bought within a set time limit.

## Formula

`Dormant ÷ Total Customer Base × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Dormant**: A customer with a prior transaction history who has not transacted again past the dormant threshold (this varies by business category, typically around 3 months for retail, 6–12 months for B2B depending on the business cycle).
- **Total Customer Base**: All customers who have ever transacted, regardless of current status, including those already dormant. See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means the risk of losing customers is rising and customer engagement is weakening.
- **A low number** means retention is healthier and the business relationship is better maintained.

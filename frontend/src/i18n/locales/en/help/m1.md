Measures the success of the **cross-selling** strategy and the depth of the business relationship with customers: how many customers are persuaded to buy more than one product category.

## Formula

`Customers Buying >1 Category ÷ Active Transacting × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Customers Buying >1 Category**: Customers who bought more than 1 product category within the measurement period (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
- **Active Transacting**: All customers who transacted in this period, including first-time customers (see the Glossary above).

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A rising number** means the cross-selling strategy is reaching more customers successfully.
- **A falling number** means customers tend to buy only 1 product category.

## General Benchmark

| Range | Tier | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| < 25% | Low | still dependent on a single product |
| 25% – 40% | Fair | a healthy baseline for an AIDC distributor |
| 40% – 60% | Good | cross-selling is working effectively |
| > 60% | Excellent | strong customer engagement and product penetration |

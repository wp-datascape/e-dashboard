Measures **customer loyalty**, shopping experience quality, and the effectiveness of retention strategy: the percentage of existing active customers who buy again after a previous transaction.

## Formula

`Customers with Repeat Orders ÷ Existing Active × 100%`

## Term Definitions

- **Customers with Repeat Orders**: Customers who made more than 1 transaction within the measurement period, or who returned to buy again after a previous transaction.
- **Existing Active**: Customers who have transacted before and are still actively transacting this period (Active Customer + Reactivated). See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** means customers are satisfied, recurring needs are being served, and the business relationship is strong.
- **A low number** may signal issues with service, product quality, or post-sale engagement.

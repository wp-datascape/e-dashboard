Measures the **business value "held up"** or at risk of being lost due to inactive customers: the total revenue or potential gross profit lost from dormant customers, out of the entire customer base.

## Formula

`Total historical value (revenue/gross profit) from all Dormant customers, within the Total Customer Base population`

## Term Definitions

- **Dormant**: A customer with a prior transaction history who has not transacted again past the dormant threshold.
- **Historical Revenue**: The average or total revenue that customer generated before becoming dormant.
- **Historical Gross Profit**: The historical gross profit that customer generated.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A high number** indicates a lot of customer value has not yet been retained or reactivated.
- **A low number** means the customer base is healthier and the risk of revenue loss is smaller.

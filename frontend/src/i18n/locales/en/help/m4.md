Measures the **profitability** of existing active customers: not just revenue, but the actual margin (gross profit) contribution generated.

## Formula

`Total Gross Profit from Existing Active ÷ Number of Existing Active, where Gross Profit = Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)`

## Term Definitions

- **Total Gross Profit from Existing Active**: Total revenue from existing active customers, minus cost of goods sold (COGS), within the measurement period.
- **Existing Active**: Customers who have transacted before and are still actively transacting this period (Active Customer + Reactivated). See the Glossary above.

## How to Read the Numbers

- **A rising number** shows existing customers are becoming more profitable, could be from buying higher-margin products, better pricing, or better cost efficiency.
- **A falling number** could mean discounts are too large, production costs rose, or the product mix shifted toward lower margins.

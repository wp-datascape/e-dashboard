Not every customer carries the same weight for your business. The **Analysis** page surfaces these priority customers first, so your attention isn't spread thin across low-value ones.

## What is a Pareto Customer

A Pareto Customer is one who contributes the most to total revenue, following the 80/20 principle: a small share of customers usually accounts for most of the revenue. These customers are marked with a **Pareto** label and are always shown first on the Analysis page.

## Steps

1. Open the **Analysis** page.
2. Look at the Customer column — customers with the **Pareto** label are your priority.
3. Turn on the **Pareto Customer** filter if you only want to see priority customers, without the rest.
4. Each row shows the revenue change compared to the previous period or last year (selectable from the Comparison dropdown), so you can immediately see which priority customers are trending up or down.

## Why this matters

When your Pareto customers start declining, the impact on overall revenue is much bigger than for a regular customer — watching them more closely helps you catch risks earlier, before they show up as a big hit on your monthly numbers.

The period granularity filter sets how wide each data point is calculated: monthly, quarterly, semesterly, or yearly. Available on the **Growth** page and the **Report** pages.

## Steps

1. Open the **Growth** page or one of the **Report** pages.
2. Find the granularity dropdown in the filter bar at the top (defaults to "Monthly").
3. Pick the granularity you need: Monthly, Quarterly, Semesterly, or Yearly.
4. The charts and numbers on the page automatically recalculate to match the selected period width.

## When to use which granularity

- **Monthly** — tracking short-term trends, good for routine weekly/monthly monitoring.
- **Quarterly / Semesterly** — routine management-level performance reviews, smooths out monthly fluctuation noise.
- **Yearly** — seeing the bigger picture year over year, good for annual reports or year-over-year comparisons.

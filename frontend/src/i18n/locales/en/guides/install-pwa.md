This dashboard can be installed as an app on your computer or phone, just like a regular app: it gets its own icon, opens without the browser's address bar, and feels faster to open compared to a regular browser tab.

## Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

1. Open the dashboard in Chrome or Edge.
2. Look for the install icon in the address bar, on the right side (a monitor icon with a down arrow).
3. Click that icon, then click **Install**.
4. If you don't see that icon, open the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner, and look for **Install Executive Dashboard...** or **Apps > Install this site as an app**.
5. The dashboard will open in its own window, separate from the browser, and appear in your computer's app list.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the dashboard in Chrome.
2. You'll usually see an automatic prompt at the bottom of the screen, "Add Executive Dashboard to Home screen" — tap **Install** or **Add**.
3. If that prompt doesn't appear, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. The dashboard icon will appear on your phone's home screen like any other app.

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

This can **only be installed through Safari** — Chrome or other browsers on iPhone cannot install web apps.

1. Open the dashboard in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (a square with an arrow pointing up), usually at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down the list of options and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner to confirm.
5. The dashboard icon will appear on your iPhone/iPad home screen.

## Why install it

- Open the dashboard straight from its icon, no need to type the address or hunt through browser tabs.
- More screen space — no browser address bar or navigation buttons in the way.
- Feels more responsive when opened from a phone, including while scrolling and opening menus.

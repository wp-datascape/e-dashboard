This dashboard uses several specific terms (Existing Active, Dormant, Customer Base, and similar) that show up repeatedly on summary cards and detail tables. All of them are explained in one place.

## Steps

1. Open the **Help** menu in the sidebar (question mark icon, at the bottom).
2. The top of the page has the **Customer Population Glossary** — an explanation of the 6 base customer statuses and the 4 combined population terms used on KPI cards.
3. Below that is an explanation for each KPI (M1 through M10): formula, term definitions, and how to read the numbers.
4. Click any KPI to open its details.

## Tip

If you're not sure which term is used by a specific KPI, check the "Population Used by Each KPI" table in the Glossary section — it lists which population term is the basis for each KPI's calculation.

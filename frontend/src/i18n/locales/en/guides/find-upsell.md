Upsell Targets helps you find customers who are likely to buy high-margin products but haven't tried them yet, based on the buying patterns of similar customers in the same business segment.

## Steps

1. Open the **Product > High Margin** page.
2. Select the **Upsell Targets** tab.
3. Each row is one customer along with the high-margin product recommended for them, together with a percentage showing how common that product is among similar customers.
4. Click a row to see the full recommendation detail and that customer's purchase history.

## How the recommendation works

1. The system looks for the best-selling high-margin products within the same business segment as that customer (a product only counts if it's been bought by at least 20 similar customers, so the recommendation reflects a real market pattern, not a coincidence).
2. The system compares those favorite products against the purchase history of the customer you're viewing, to find products they haven't bought yet.

## How to read the numbers

The percentage on each recommendation (for example, "Scanner — 48.7%") means 48.7% of customers in the same business type have bought that category, while the customer you're viewing hasn't. The higher the percentage, the stronger the case for offering that product to them.

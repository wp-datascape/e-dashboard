The accent color sets the main color used across the dashboard: buttons, charts, active icons, and page titles.

## Steps

1. Open the **Settings** menu in the sidebar, then select **App Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Accent Color** section.
3. Click one of the available color swatches. The active one is marked with a check.
4. The change applies instantly across the whole dashboard, no page reload needed.

## Note

Your color choice is saved to your account, so it stays applied even when you log in from another device. Accent color is separate from light/dark mode — both settings can be configured independently on the same page.
